# PhysicsX

> PhysicsX is an AI-native engineering software company (London / New York, founded 2020, born out of Formula One) building a platform that embeds physics AI into industrial engineering and manufacturing workflows. It unifies simulation, physics AI, data, and engineering applications into one foundation, pairing fast AI-driven physics inference (Deep Physics Models) with numerical simulation. There is no public developer API, SDK, or documentation surface at this time.
